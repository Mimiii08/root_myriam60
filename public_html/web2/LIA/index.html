AMARULA

OUR STORY

Amarula was born from a love for African nature, culture, and connection. Inspired by the powerful Drakensberg Mountains, our brand blends the spirit of South Africa with the rich flavors of fruits from across the continent. Each bottle is a tribute to unity, creativity, and the natural beauty of Africa. We work closely with communities in different African countries to source unique, high-quality fruits that reflect the diversity of the continent. From smooth cream liqueurs to bold fruit blends, Amarula brings people together through taste, tradition, and innovation.

OUR PURPOSE

At Amarula, our purpose goes beyond creating a great drink. We believe in building something that gives back. That’s why we focus on working with local farmers and small producers across Africa, sourcing fruits directly from the communities that grow them. By supporting these farmers, we help create jobs, encourage fair trade, and celebrate the richness of African agriculture. Each bottle of Amarula represents more than flavor — it’s a step toward sustainable growth, cultural pride, and shared success across the continent.
