Project description

Systemic barriers weigh heavily on marginalized communities, limiting access to education, jobs, housing, and essential resources. Built through generations of inequity, these obstacles continue to block opportunities and create cycles of exclusion. The Price of Colour is a student run campaign in collaboration with Welfare Avenue that aims to shed light on these hidden struggles, showing that the weight of the system should not define a community’s future. In collaboration with Welfare Avenue, this campaign empowers individuals to take action by turning awareness into collective efforts to break down barriers and build a more equitable future. The weight is heavy, but together, we can break it.

My role

In this campaign, I helped with both the video production and the creation of graphic posters. I worked with the team to film key moments for the video , making sure the visuals matched the campaign's goal of focusing on systemic barriers faced by POCs communities. For the posters, I designed visuals on Adobe Illustrator, using strong imagery and clear messaging to raise awareness . I also collaborated closely with the team to ensure everything was aligned with the overall vision, making quick adjustments when needed to meet deadlines. This project gave me great hands-on experience in video production, design, and teamwork, all while contributing to a good cause.
